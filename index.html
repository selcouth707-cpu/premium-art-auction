Auction Rules & Policies

Transparency and trust are at the heart of everything we do

Auction Rules

  • Bidding Process: All bids are binding. Once placed, a bid cannot be retracted except in cases of clear error or fraud.
  • Bid Increments: Minimum bid increments are set at 5% of the current bid amount.
  • Reserve Price: Some artworks have a reserve price. If bidding does not reach this amount, the artwork will not be sold.
  • Auction Extension: If a bid is placed in the final 2 minutes, the auction will automatically extend by 2 minutes.
  • Winner Notification: Winning bidders will be notified via email within 1 hour of auction close.
  • Payment Terms: Full payment is due within 48 hours of auction close. Accepted methods: Wire transfer, credit card, cryptocurrency.
  • Buyer's Premium: A 15% buyer's premium is added to the final hammer price.

Refund Policy

  • Authenticity Guarantee: If an artwork is proven to be inauthentic within 5 years of purchase, we offer a full refund including buyer's premium.
  • Condition Disputes: Claims regarding condition must be made within 7 days of delivery with photographic evidence.
  • Damage in Transit: All shipments are fully insured. Report any damage within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Refund Processing: Approved refunds are processed within 10-14 business days to the original payment method.
  • Non-Refundable Items: Buyer's premium and shipping costs are non-refundable except in cases of authenticity disputes.
  • Return Shipping: Buyers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is due to our error or damage.

Seller Fees & Monetization

  • Standard Listing: Free for approved artists. 10% commission on successful sales.
  • Premium Listing: $99/month. Includes featured placement, priority support, and reduced commission (7%).
  • Featured Auction: $299 per auction. Homepage placement, email marketing to 50K+ collectors, social media promotion.
  • Payment Schedule: Sellers receive payment within 7 business days after buyer payment clears.
  • Withdrawal Methods: Bank transfer, PayPal, or cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum).

Privacy & Data Protection

We are committed to protecting your personal information. All data is encrypted and stored securely. We never sell your information to third parties. User activity is tracked for analytics purposes to improve our platform. IP addresses and timestamps are logged for security and fraud prevention.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our platform

How do I register as a buyer? +
Click the "Register" button in the navigation menu and complete the buyer registration form. You'll need to provide your name, email, phone number, and agree to our terms. Optional ID verification is available for higher bidding limits. Your account will be reviewed and approved within 24 hours.
How do I apply as an artist? +
Navigate to "Apply as Artist" and submit your application including personal details, portfolio, artist statement, and bank information. Our curation team reviews all applications within 5-7 business days. Approved artists receive access to list artworks and participate in auctions.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept wire transfers, major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), and cryptocurrencies (Bitcoin, Ethereum). All transactions are processed securely through encrypted payment gateways.
How is shipping handled? +
We partner with specialized art logistics companies to ensure safe delivery. All shipments are fully insured and tracked. Shipping costs are calculated based on artwork size, weight, and destination. International shipping is available to most countries.
What if I win an auction but change my mind? +
All bids are legally binding. Failure to complete payment may result in account suspension and legal action. We recommend carefully reviewing artwork details before bidding. Contact support immediately if you have concerns.
How do you verify artwork authenticity? +
Every artwork undergoes rigorous authentication by our expert team. We provide certificates of authenticity, provenance documentation, and artist verification. Our 5-year authenticity guarantee protects your investment.
