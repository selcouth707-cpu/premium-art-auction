Biography

Sarah Mitchell is an internationally recognized abstract artist whose work explores the intersection of color, form, and emotion. With over 15 years of practice, her distinctive style has been featured in galleries across North America and Europe.

Her work is characterized by bold brushstrokes, vibrant color palettes, and a deep understanding of composition. Sarah holds an MFA from the Rhode Island School of Design and has received numerous awards for her contributions to contemporary art.

Certificates & Awards

  • 2022 - Excellence in Abstract Art, National Gallery Award
  • 2021 - MFA, Rhode Island School of Design
  • 2020 - Emerging Artist Grant, Arts Foundation

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

How do online auctions work? +
Artworks are offered for a fixed auction period. Collectors place bids, and the highest bid at the close of the auction secures the artwork.
Are bids binding? +
Yes. All bids placed are final and legally binding.
Can an auction be extended? +
To ensure fair participation, auctions may be extended briefly in the event of last-minute bidding.
What payment methods are accepted? +
We accept selected payment methods including credit/debit cards, bank transfers, PayPal, and approved digital assets.
When must payment be completed? +
Successful bidders are required to complete payment within 48–72 hours of auction close.
Are there additional fees? +
Buyers are responsible for shipping, insurance, and applicable taxes or duties unless otherwise stated.
How is artwork shipped? +
Artworks are carefully packaged and shipped either by the artist or through our logistics partners.
Is artwork insured during transit? +
Basic transit insurance is included. Additional insurance may be arranged upon request.
When will my artwork arrive? +
Delivery timelines vary based on location and artwork specifications. Estimated timelines are shared after purchase.
Are artworks authentic? +
All artworks are submitted by artists or lawful owners. Documentation such as certificates of authenticity may be provided where applicable.
What if an artwork is damaged or misrepresented? +
In rare cases of verified damage or authenticity concerns, the matter will be reviewed and resolved in accordance with our policies.
Who can submit artwork? +
Original artists or lawful owners may submit works for curatorial review.
Is submission guaranteed to be accepted? +
No. All submissions are carefully reviewed, and acceptance is at the platform's discretion.
When do sellers receive payment? +
Seller payouts are released after buyer payment confirmation and successful delivery.
Is my personal information secure? +
Yes. All personal data is handled securely and in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
Can my account be suspended? +
Accounts may be restricted or suspended in cases of non-payment, policy violations, or fraudulent activity.
How can I contact support? +
For any assistance, please contact us via the Contact section or email our support team at support@artehaus.com.
